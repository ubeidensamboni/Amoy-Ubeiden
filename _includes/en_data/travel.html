Plane

If you're traveling from abroad, you'll want to fly into either Alfonso Bonilla Aragón International Airport (CLO) in Cali or El Edén International Airport (AXM) in Armenia.

We recommend renting a car if your budget allows — it's the most flexible way to get around. CLO is about 126 km from Roldanillo (roughly 2 to 2 hours and 45 minutes by car), while AXM is much closer at just 58 km, making it the more convenient option.

Need help with transportation plans? Just let us know — we're happy to assist!
