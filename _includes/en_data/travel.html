Travel

Plane

The closest airports are Alfonso Bonilla Aragón International Airport (CLO) in Cali and El Edén International Airport (AXM) in Armenia. For more details about the closest airport to the venue and the venue location, CLICK HERE .

For our international guests, we will provide transportation from the airport to the accommodation on Friday, 23rd of October, and from the accommodation to the airport on Monday, 26th of October. If you prefer to explore on your own, rental cars are available (see information below).

Before booking your travel, please contact us to let us know the time and the airport that works best for you. We will then organize a group transport that fits the majority. Please do it before the 24th of July 2026.

We will try our best to accommodate everyone. If you cannot land/depart at the same day/time as everyone else, we will help you organize another way of getting to and from the venue.
