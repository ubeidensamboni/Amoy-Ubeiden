Frequently Asked Questions

Esto es una prueba....Can I bring a date/plus one?

Unfortunately, we have a strict guest list to stay on budget. We will only be able to accommodate those listed on your wedding invitation.

Will transportation be arranged?

We are happy to offer transportation for our families and friends arriving from different places. Rides will be available from the airport to the accommodation and back.

What time should I arrive at the ceremony?

Our ceremony will begin promptly at 3:00 pm. This will allow you plenty of time to park and find a seat!

Do you have any recommendations for where to eat, points of interest or things to do?

Yes! We recommend visiting the ‘Things to Do’ section, where we’ve compiled all of our favourite local hot spots and fun things to do in the area, including coffee shops, spas and restaurants to visit before or after the wedding.

What will the weather be like?

In Roldanillo the temperature generally varies from 19°C to 30°C and rarely drops below 18°C.

About Money

Most shops in Roldanillo only accept cash, so we recommend exchanging $400–500 USD at the airport for travel and food. If you need more cash later, you can use the Western Union app to send money to yourself—there are plenty of locations in town to pick it up.
